✳   GOOD TO KNOW

A few little
questions.

Everything you might want to know as you get to know Pawlio.

What is Pawlio?+

Pawlio is a pet care companion for keeping your pets’ profiles, care records, routines and reminders together.

Can I keep track of more than one pet?+

Yes. Each pet can have their own profile so their information stays organized and easy to find.

What kinds of pets is Pawlio for?+

Pawlio is designed for a range of companions, including dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, fish, reptiles and more.

What can I keep track of?+

You can organize health records, grooming, meals, training routines and reminders for your pets.
